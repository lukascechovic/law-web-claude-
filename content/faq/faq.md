## What materials are Lukas Archery Works products made from?

The ARC sideback quiver is built from traditional beef leather for durability and a classic feel. WINGS, the arrow nocking aid, is made from renewable bioplastic and tuned to the arrow diameter. The focus across the range is on functionality, comfort, and ease of maintenance.

## Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Every piece is made to order, and the gear is designed to be easily adjustable and modified to suit each rider's needs. Use the contact form or the chatbot to discuss your requirements and we will tailor the build accordingly.

## Where do you ship, and how do I order?

Lukas Archery Works ships from Slovakia across Europe and beyond. The site is inquiry-driven rather than a checkout — get in touch through the contact form or the chatbot and we will confirm availability, lead time, and shipping for your location.

## Which nocking techniques do your quivers support?

It depends on the product. The ARC sideback quiver supports both the Slavic technique and the Thumb technique. The HORIZON supports the Slavic technique. Each product states its own supported nocking techniques, so check the product you are interested in rather than assuming they match.

## What is the difference between the ARC and the HORIZON?

The ARC is a traditional beef-leather sideback quiver offered in 30- or 45-arrow variants, supporting both Slavic and Thumb techniques. The HORIZON is a modern, highly modular single-row quiver holding up to 31 arrows, positioned as the fastest quiver and the successor to the legacy Flying quiver; it supports the Slavic technique. Both are quivers, just built for different priorities.

## What exactly is WINGS?

WINGS is an arrow nocking aid — a small precision part that makes seating an arrow on the bowstring secure and fast. It is the brand's only non-quiver product, made from renewable bioplastic and tuned per arrow diameter. It is a nocking aid, not the arrow's nock itself.
