title: How It Is Made
steps:
  - title: Design & CAD
    description: >-
      Every part begins as a precise digital model. We refine each curve and
      tolerance in CAD until the geometry fits the rider, the bow, and the
      demands of horseback archery — long before a single piece is produced.
    image: process-01.webp
  - title: Bioplastic, Made On Demand
    description: >-
      Parts such as the WINGS nocking aid are produced on demand from renewable
      bioplastic. Nothing sits in a warehouse: each piece is made when it is
      ordered, cutting waste and letting us tune the part to your arrow diameter.
    image: process-02.webp
  - title: Leatherwork By Hand
    description: >-
      The ARC and HORIZON quivers are cut, stitched, and finished from
      traditional beef leather by hand. Decades of craft go into seams that
      survive the saddle, season after season.
    image: process-03.webp
  - title: Finishing & Quality Control
    description: >-
      Before anything ships, every product is inspected by the maker — edges
      burnished, hardware checked, fit verified. If it would not pass on the
      track, it does not leave the workshop.
